### Decisions

- **Age and consent.** Sign up asks birth month and year. Under 12 is pointed to Amai Learn. 12 to 17 need guardian consent before studying (`requireLearner`). The learner makes a link and sends it (WhatsApp share built in); the guardian opens it on their own phone, confirms they are the parent or guardian and 18 or older, gives a name, relationship and contact, and agrees to `CONSENT_TEXT_VERSION` 2026-10-29. The same link withdraws consent for 400 days. Only the link's SHA 256 hash is stored. Verification is the separate device and the recorded contact; no email or SMS is sent yet (no provider configured). This is a reasonable first step, to be checked against the gazetted Act before launch (SPEC.md 12).
- **No consent, no data.** An under 18 account whose guardian never consents is deleted after 30 days by the retention sweep.
- **Prefilter retuning.** Biology, history and literature words pass (tested). Redirected for everyone: explicit content, romance and companion roleplay, weapons, violence instructions, getting drugs, rule overrides. Redirected for under 18s only: alcohol and smoking, betting apps, secrets from guardians. Personal details are flagged for under 18s only. Distress always wins and opens Get help.
- **Get help** lists only Childline 116, which is verified (UNICEF Zimbabwe, findahelpline.com). WhatsApp numbers and adult hotlines are left out until verified from an official source.
- **Study styles** are applied as `data-style` on `<html>`; accessibility settings as `data-dyslexia`, `data-large`, `data-contrast`, `data-motion`. "Read aloud" and "Shorter chunks" are stored now and used from Phase 2, when Taku answers.
- **Copy rule** is enforced by keeping all UI copy in `src/copy/en.ts` and the consent text in `convex/accounts/consentText.ts`, both tested for hyphens and dashes.
- **Default model** stays Amayi Learn's checked chain until live calls start; re check on that day.
- **Icons** are drawn in code (`public/icon.svg` and PNGs): a geometric mark, not a generated image.
- **Local e2e** uses an anonymous local Convex backend (`CONVEX_AGENT_MODE=anonymous`). The CLI's optional dashboard download fails through this network's proxy; a cached dashboard config skips it.

### Decisions

- **Search without embeddings (D8, for now).** Notebook chunks use a Convex full text search index filtered by notebook. A notebook of 12 chunks or fewer is sent whole. This needs no extra AI provider and costs nothing per question. Embeddings can be added later if answers miss relevant pages.
- **Reading costs nothing where it can.** Pasted text, links, Word files and PDFs with a text layer are read with no AI call (pdf.js in a Node action). Photos and PDFs that are mostly scans go to the model (`source_read`), which costs money; they are capped at 20 pages per source.
- **Quote check.** Every cited part must carry a 5 to 25 word quote. The server normalises words (case, punctuation, quotes) and accepts the quote when at least 85 percent of its words match in place on the cited page, which allows a few misread words in photos. Quotes under 3 words never count.
- **Source modes.** Only my material drops every part whose quote fails, and the answer says how many points were left out and what is missing. My material plus Taku's ideas keeps them, marked "Taku's idea, not from your notes". New notebooks start in My material plus Taku's ideas.
- **Answer mode** comes from the learner's profile and can be switched on the notebook screen. Guide me tells Taku to give one hint or question at a time and never a finished essay. The held answer filter for maths comes with the maths check layer in Phase 5.
- **Safety.** The prefilter runs on every question. Distress opens Get help with no model call. Under 18s: personal details are not sent to any model, and a fast model input check runs first and fails closed. Adults: prefilter only, to keep cost down; revisit with the red team in Phase 3.
- **Copy rule in answers.** Taku is told not to use em or en dashes, and the server replaces any that slip through with commas.
- **Links** are fetched server side: http and https only, no local or private addresses, HTML and plain text only, 2 MB cap.
- **The chat belongs to the learner**: questions and answers are stored in the notebook and deleted with it or with the account.
- A newer Chromium returns a promise from `scrollIntoView`; effects in this app use braces so they never return a value.

### Decisions

- **Tutor name:** Taku. It matches the code. The audit raised Chris; no rename was needed.
- **Link reader (A1):** every hop goes through `linkProblem` plus an address check in the connection's own DNS lookup, so a host cannot resolve to a public address for the check and a private one for the fetch. Three hops at most. The fetch moved to a `"use node"` action, because only Node exposes that lookup.
- **Email codes (A2):** Convex Auth email codes through Resend. Three modes in `convex/accounts/mail.ts`: `resend` when `RESEND_API_KEY` is set, `mock` when `AI_MODE=mock`, otherwise `off`. Off means sign up works with no verification and no password reset, so deploying before the key exists locks nobody out. A real account's mail is never sent in mock mode; codes go to a mock outbox that refuses writes outside mock mode.
- **requireAdmin (A2)** now needs a verified email as well as a listed one. No function calls it yet, so nothing changes today.
- **Google sign in (A3):** configured only when both `AUTH_GOOGLE_ID` and `AUTH_GOOGLE_SECRET` are set; the button is hidden otherwise. Only id, email and verified flag are taken from Google's profile.
- **Guardian consent (A4):** consent needs the 6 digit code. Codes are hashed with the request and phone, so a code works only for the phone it went to. Five sends a day per learner and per phone, one per minute, five tries, ten minutes. The link stays. **Consequence: with no SMS provider, a new under 18 cannot finish consent in production.** The guardian's page says so plainly. This follows the brief ("consent completes only with code", "real provider unconfigured"). Limit of the check, raised again by the security review: it proves control of a phone, not who the person is. A learner holds the link and could type their own number. Decide how to handle this before adding a real SMS provider.
- **Per learner USD cap (B5):** call counts are gone. `LEARNER_DAILY_USD_CAP` defaults to USD 0.05 a day. **That number is an assumption, not a measurement.** `AI_DAILY_USD_CAP` keeps its USD 0.50 default; at that level very few learners can use the app in one day. Takunda should set both; production settings were not touched here. Cost is recorded per learner, per deployment and per tool; alerts are rows in `spendAlerts` at 50 and 80 percent, read with `npx convex run ai/usage:alerts`.
- **Parallel calls and the caps (security review):** cost is known only after a model answers, so parallel calls could all pass the check. `admit` is a mutation, so Convex runs each in turn, and it limits starts: 12 a minute per learner, 20 a minute for reading photos and scans. This bounds the overshoot rather than ending it: a learner can pass the cap by what one minute of starts costs, and many learners at once can pass the deployment cap by a little. A reservation system would close it fully; not worth the cost at this size.
- **HTML to text (security review):** rewritten as one pass with indexOf, because the old regexes went quadratic on 2 MB of open tags from a hostile page. An unclosed tag now drops the rest of the page.
- **Model chains (B5):** `MODEL_MAIN` and `MODEL_FAST` take comma lists. The default chain is unchanged; no new ids were invented. Fallback across providers is still limited by the zero data retention provider rule in the router.
- **Mock mode is priced.** Mock calls cost the default model's price for the fixture's token counts, so caps and alerts can be exercised. These are modelled numbers, never a real cost.
- **Second opinion (B6):** fast tier, on Taku's ideas in summaries, flashcards and answers, and on quiz marking schemes. Rejected items are dropped. If the check itself fails, items stay marked "not checked" and a paper shows a note: a failed check should not empty a paper. It is skipped for Guide me hints, where the extra call buys little. It catches errors a second model can see, and it is not proof of correctness.
- **Maths (C7):** LaTeX between $ signs with a money guard ("$5 and $10" stays text). KaTeX and mhchem load only when a page has maths. `trust: false` and `throwOnError: false`, so source text can never make a link or crash a page.
- **Widgets (C8):** packages under `widgets/<slug>/`, same shape as `modes/`. Taku supplies data; the app renders and a deterministic checker marks. Equation steps uses mathjs with a whitelist of node types (nothing else is evaluated) and a hint that states the answer is removed. Long division takes only two numbers from Taku and works out every step itself. Fill in the blanks drops any blank whose quote is not on the cited page or does not hold the answer, whatever the source mode. The checkers are shared TypeScript in `convex/widgets/`, used by server and browser.
- **Streaming (D11):** the chat row exists from the start and is patched as text arrives, using Convex subscriptions rather than an HTTP stream, which keeps auth and storage in one place. Rows left unfinished for more than three minutes show as failed on the client. A half written widget block never shows.
- **Sign out (D10):** Settings. Guardian, Setup and too young screens keep their own button, since Settings cannot be reached from them.
- **Landing (D15):** the page's HTML is rendered into `dist/index.html` at build time, and the build fails if the copy goes missing. It is removed before paint for anyone with a saved session or on another address. `/art/og-default.webp` comes from the art batch; WebP support in link preview crawlers varies and has not been tested, because nothing is deployed.
- **Sentry (E16):** loaded only when `VITE_SENTRY_DSN` is set. No user info, no breadcrumbs, no bodies, and guardian link tokens are cut from URLs before an event leaves.
- **Analytics (E16):** a daily count per event in this deployment's own table, with no vendor, no user id and no content. Off until an adult switches it on; the server refuses under 18s whatever the browser sends.
- **Privacy notice and terms (E17):** drafts, marked "Draft for legal review" on the page, written only from what the code does. They claim compliance with no law. A lawyer has not read them.
- **Red team (E18):** the mock run proves the app's own layers route each case; its replies were written by hand, so it says nothing about what a model would do. **The live run has not been done.** No held answer output filter was added; that is still Phase 5.
- **Attribution:** commits in this work carry no co author line, because the brief forbids model identifiers in commits.

### Follow ups

- Accounts that sign up and never verify are not swept yet.
- Choose an SMS provider and implement `SmsSender`.
- Measure real cost per learner, then set the caps from data.
- Run `pnpm redteam --cap 0.10` before under 18s use the app.

## Stage 1: hardening (16 November 2026)

Scope: docs/AUDIT-2026-11-16.md items S1, S3, S4, S5, S6, S7, S9, S10, S11, U1, U2, U7. No new features. Source commit for reference reading: amayi-learn `290e4b4`. Nothing was ported in this stage.

### Decisions

- **Batched deletes (S1).** `convex/privacy/purge.ts`. A delete marks the row `deletingAt` in the calling mutation (every query skips it; an account's sign in rows go in the same mutation and the user row loses its email), then empties it 200 rows per mutation, each step scheduling the next. The first step runs inline, so small deletes still finish at once. The retention sweep starts one account per step. A step interrupted by a deploy is picked up by the daily sweep (`purge.resume`). The 200 row step is a choice, not a measured limit: a chunk with its embedding is about 6 KB, so a step reads about 1.2 MB against Convex's per mutation limits.
- **Approved by Takunda on 16 November 2026:** 30 days after withdrawal, 500 MB per account, and the high default price for unknown models. They are decisions now, not assumptions.
- **Uploads (S3).** `convex/notebooks/uploads.ts`. `generateUploadUrl` returns an upload address and a ticket. `addFile` needs the ticket: the caller's own, unused, under an hour old, and the file not already held by any ticket or source. A stored file's id is only known after the upload, so the binding is ticket then claim; the remaining gap is a file between upload and claim, reachable only by someone who holds both a ticket of their own and the unguessable storage id. Limits: 30 tickets a minute, 60 open tickets, `STORAGE_QUOTA_MB` (default 500) per account. **The 500 MB default is an assumption.** A failed validation returns a message and gives the file back; it no longer throws, because a throw would undo the cleanup. The hourly sweep walks `_storage` oldest first and deletes files over an hour old that no ticket and no source holds; a source's file counts as held even without a ticket, so files from before this stage are safe before `uploads:backfill` runs.
- **Withdrawn consent (S4).** The account and everything in it are deleted 30 days after withdrawal (`WITHDRAWN_DAYS`). **30 days is a choice to be checked against the Act and with a lawyer.** The guardian's link now offers deletion at once; it works only where a guardian gave consent on that link with the phone code, because the learner holds the link too.
- **Unverified accounts.** Swept 14 days after sign up when the email was never confirmed and the account holds no notebook and no chat. Runs only while email codes can be sent (`mailMode` is not off): today production has no email key, so this rule does nothing there until the key is set, and it never deletes an account that has work in it.
- **Input check for everyone (S5).** The fast model input check now runs on every question and chat message, whatever birth year was given. It costs one fast call per message. The stricter rules (personal details, extra prefilter categories, output check, no streaming) stay for declared under 18s. School attested ages wait for school licences.
- **Under 18 output (S6).** Chat replies for under 18s are not streamed; the finished reply, and every notebook answer and study tool, passes a fast output check (`convex/safety/checks.ts`, prompt `safety-output.md`) or is not shown. No clear allow means not shown. Adults keep streaming and have no output check.
- **Under 18 sources (S6).** Screened as they are read (`convex/notebooks/read.ts screenPages`): the word filter runs on every page with no model call; only pages it flags go to the fast model (`safety-source.md`). More than 8 flagged pages refuses the source without a model call. Pasted text from an under 18 now goes through a scheduled step instead of being saved in the same mutation. Adults' material is not screened. Limit: the word filter was written for short messages, so it can miss a harmful page that uses none of its words; the output check on answers is the second net.
- **Security headers (S7).** `scripts/lib/security-headers.mjs` makes them; `vercel.json` carries the same list (tested), and `scripts/deploy-vercel.mjs` writes them into the prebuilt output with the deployment's exact Convex origin over https and wss. `script-src` is `'self'` plus the hash of the one inline script. `style-src` keeps `'unsafe-inline'` because KaTeX and the art set styles on elements. `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` everywhere, which covers the consent link. `pnpm csp-check` serves the built site with the headers and drives sign up, a cited answer, an upload, a chat with maths and the privacy page in a browser, failing on any console error or policy violation: the e2e journeys run on the Vite dev server, which cannot carry the policy.
- **Usage counters (S9).** The deployment total and each tool's total are written to one of 16 shard rows picked at random; `record` reads only the rows it writes. Readers add the shards and any row from before sharding. Spend alerts moved from `record` to `admit`, where the total is read anyway. `analyticsDaily` was left as it is: it is written only for consenting adults and is far from contention.
- **Unknown model price (S10).** USD 15 per million input tokens and USD 75 per million output tokens, used only when a model has no price row and the provider reports no cost. **These numbers are a deliberately high guess, not a quoted price.**
- **Chat list (S11).** Paginated, 50 a page at most; the chat screen has "Show older chats".
- **Tools cover what was chosen (U1).** `generate` takes a scope: sources, each whole or from one page to another. Chapters are found from headings ("Chapter 3", "Unit 2", "Topic", "Module", "Section", "Part" at the top of a page); a long source without headings is offered in blocks of 20 pages. The chosen pages are read in sections of 30,000 characters, one model call per section, and the results are merged in code (no merging model call); items and minutes are shared out across sections. At most 8 sections per tool, and the run stops early at the spending caps; either way the tool says "This covers part of what you chose". Every tool stores and shows the pages it used ("Made from pages 1 to 40 of Biology.pdf"). Widgets still read the first pages only: they practise one thing.
- **Embeddings (U2, SPEC D8).** Route chosen on 16 November 2026 from OpenRouter's public lists (no call was made): `openai/text-embedding-3-small` on the `azure` provider, which the zero data retention endpoint list (`/api/v1/endpoints/zdr`) carries for that model, at USD 0.02 per million tokens on the embeddings model list. Calls send `provider.only: ["azure"]`, `allow_fallbacks: false`, `data_collection: "deny"`, `zdr: true`. Vectors are cut to their first 512 numbers and rescaled, which this model family is trained to allow, to keep a chunk's row near 6 KB. `EMBED_MODEL` and `EMBED_PROVIDER` override the route. Search is hybrid: the keyword index and the vector index each give 16 matches, merged by reciprocal rank fusion, top 8 chunks. A notebook of 12 chunks or fewer is still sent whole. Mock mode makes vectors from the words alone (a hashed bag of words), the one place the router answers without a fixture. **Not verified live:** that the route answers, the cost, and that search by meaning improves answers; all three need a capped live run. Embedding cost counts against the learner's and the deployment's daily caps; when a cap stops it, the daily backfill tries again.
- **Embeddings switch.** `EMBEDDINGS_MODE` (Convex env, default off). Off: keyword search only, no embedding calls, the backfill does nothing. Tests and e2e set it on (fake vectors cost nothing). Production turns it on only after `ai/embeddings:liveCheck` passes with a cap of USD 0.10, as Takunda asked.
- **Several files (U7).** A drop or a multiple pick adds up to 20 files, one after another; one bad file does not stop the rest.
- **Local backend.** A local Convex backend left running since 6 November from the sibling folder `taku-dzidza-build` held port 3210. It was stopped so this folder's backend could start. Its data is on disk and was not touched.

### Production pointed at the wrong backend (found 16 November 2026)

The site published on 13 November (`index-BSaZHnIA.js`) was built with `VITE_CONVEX_URL=https://happy-otter-123.convex.cloud`, the example address in the Convex client library's own docs, which answers 404. Production is `https://disciplined-condor-946.eu-west-1.convex.cloud`. From that deploy until the Stage 1 deploy, nobody could sign in or sign up on takudzidza.vercel.app; the landing and sign in pages loaded, so the live check of that day did not show it. The likely cause: the URL was read from the old bundle with a pattern that allowed no dot in the deployment name, so it skipped the real address (which has a region, `.eu-west-1.`) and matched the example string instead. Fix: the build now takes the URL from `convex deploy` itself (`scripts/build-site.mjs` writes `.cache/convex-url`), refuses a bundle that does not contain that exact URL or still contains the local one, and the deploy script uses that file for the security headers. Nothing reads the URL back from a bundle any more.

### Deploying without tokens

`pnpm deploy:prod` now uses the stored Convex and Vercel CLI logins when no tokens are set (Convex `CONVEX_DEPLOYMENT=prod:disciplined-condor-946`; Vercel project `prj_VXSP2nXy5izpDyRZydbsIIe41bV5` in team `team_1VHQZXdpbaqhVdy5JbGxZHOq`). `pnpm deploy:prod --dry-run` confirms both targets and changes nothing.

### After the deploy, once

- `npx convex run notebooks/uploads:backfill` gives each account its byte count from files it already holds.
- `npx convex run ai/embeddings:backfill` embeds existing chunks (it also runs daily). It spends money: about USD 0.02 per million tokens, counted against the caps.

## Stage 2: teacher foundation (16 November 2026)

### Provenance

amayi-learn `convex/teach`, `src/grown`, `src/office` and `src/worksheet` are Stage 3 material (schemes, plans, term tests, Word and PDF export) and were read, not ported, here.

### Decisions

- **Roles on one account.** `profiles.roles` (optional): teacher, guardian, school admin. Every account is a learner. Only adults can teach or be a guardian. Teaching is a switch in Settings; guardian is added when an adult first makes a child code; school admin when they make a school.
- **Teachers see first names only.** The first word of the display name. Never an email, a birth date or anything the learner studies on their own. Results by topic are counts and percentages with no names.
- **Guardian approval cannot be done by the learner.** The consent link is held by the learner too (they made it). So an answer from the link needs a fresh code sent to the phone the guardian verified when giving consent (`accounts/consent:sendApprovalCode`, `decideClass`). A guardian account can answer with one tap only for a child whose standing consent that account gave while signed in (`consents.guardianProfileId`); a child who makes a second adult account and links it gets no say. Without an SMS provider in production, the link route waits for SMS and the account route works.
- **Consent once.** A guardian adds a child with a one time code (hashed, 7 days, 10 tries an hour per child account), then gives permission once from their family page, signed in, with the usual phone code. After that, class requests are one tap.
- **Sharing is view only, of the teacher's own notebooks.** Summaries and mind maps, and source titles. Quizzes and papers reach a class only as set work, so the scheme is never shown before hand in. ## Course packs (21 November 2026)

- **What a pack is** (`coursePacks`, `convex/packs`): an organisation's teacher or admin picks one of their own notebooks, names the pack (title, subject, level, optional syllabus id) and publishes it. The notebook stays theirs and is the pack's master; it cannot be deleted while it is linked to the pack. Everything stays behind TEACHER_MODE.
- **Publishing** (`packs/publish:publish`). The sources were read and embedded once, when the teacher added them. Publishing copies them into the pack's own notebook (see "Packs belong to the organisation" below), then makes the summary, flashcards (5, medium) and practice paper (4 questions, medium, 15 minutes) once from that copy, with the normal study tools, charged to whoever publishes, so the cost and the daily allowance are theirs. Each publish is a new version; the pack records the version's source ids and tool ids. A lock stops two publishes running at once.
- **Rights.** Publishing needs a ticked box: "I own these notes or am licensed to share them. They are not pages from a published book." Publishing is refused while the notebook holds any source marked as published book pages (`thirdParty`), and such a source is never read from a pack even if one is marked later.
- **Adding a pack** (`packs/packs:add`): anyone who can see the organisation (listed or checked, or a member) can add a published pack. They get their own notebook with `packId`. Nothing is copied: the notebook lists the pack's sources and tools first, marked "From the course pack", read only; Ask Taku and the learner's own study tools search the pack's published sources alongside anything the learner adds; citations open the pack's pages. The learner's questions, notes, tools, marks and review cards stay theirs. The one thing held per learner is a review card for each pack flashcard, because the schedule is theirs.
- **New versions** reach every linked notebook at once, because each reads the pack's current version. When a linked notebook opens on a newer version, its review cards are brought up to it (`packs/packs:sync`): cards for the new flashcards, keeping the schedule of any card whose front is unchanged, and cards from older versions removed. A practice paper attempt on a pack paper is kept in the learner's notebook.
- **Teachers see counts only**: drafts, published versions and how many learners added each pack, never who, nor anything they asked or scored.
- **The Takudzidza organisation.** An account on ADMIN_EMAILS opens it from the owner page (`organisations/owner:takudzidza`): made once, checked, the owner as admin. Packs there follow the same rules, from the owner's own notebooks, so they hold only what the owner uploads. Its place is Harare, Zimbabwe, where Takunda works.
- **Deletion.** Deleting a learner's account or linked notebook removes their review cards and paper attempts on pack tools and leaves the pack. What happens to the pack itself is in "Packs belong to the organisation" below; it replaces the earlier rule that deleting a publisher's account removed their packs. An owner merge moves packs, and the notebooks that hold them, with the organisation.
- **Schema.** New table `coursePacks`; `notebooks.packId` and `notebooks.packVersion` are optional, with an index `by_pack`. Nothing existing is rewritten.

## Packs belong to the organisation, and are checked for under 18s (21 November 2026)

- **Ownership.** A course pack belongs to its organisation, not to the teacher who made it. At publish the master notebook's sources are copied, with their pages, search chunks and embeddings, into a notebook owned by the organisation (`notebooks.organisationId`, no `profileId`); the pack points at it (`coursePacks.notebookId`). Its sources and tools carry no account either, and the pack's own flashcard cards seed learners' cards without being anyone's review queue. A source already copied (`sources.copiedFrom`) is not copied again; a copy of a source the teacher has since removed goes when the next version publishes. The uploaded file stays with the teacher's source: learners only ever read pages, so the copy does not need it, and no account's storage counts the copy.
- **Later versions** are made from that copy. While the master exists and its teacher is still on the organisation's staff, a new version first brings the copy up to date with the master; after that, versions are made from the copy as it stands.
- **Who can change a pack** (`packs/packs:managedPack`): any admin of the organisation, and the teacher who made it while they are still a teacher or admin there, can publish, unpublish (take it off the page; publishing again brings it back as a new version) or edit its name, subject, level and syllabus. Other teachers see it with its learner count only.
- **Leaving and deletion.** A teacher leaving the organisation keeps the pack with the organisation and unlinks it from their notebook, which they can then delete. Deleting a teacher's account keeps the organisation's packs and clears the pack's link to the account (`createdBy` and `masterNotebookId` are unset). Only deleting the organisation removes its packs, with the notebooks that hold their sources and tools (today that happens to a pending organisation nobody else belongs to, when the account that added it is deleted). Learners' linked notebooks then show only what each learner added.
- **The under 18 check at publish** (`convex/safety/minorCheck.ts`). A teacher is an adult, so nothing was checked when they made their notes and tools, but learners who add a pack may be under 18. Every pack source gets the source screen (`screenForMinor`, the core of `screenPages` in `convex/notebooks/read.ts`: the free word filter on every page, the model on flagged pages only) before any tool is made, and every saved pack tool gets the under 18 output check (`outputAllowed`). Each runs once per item: the verdict is kept on the row (`minorCheck`), a copy keeps its source's verdict, and a source read again loses it. Calls are charged to whoever publishes. If anything fails, or cannot be checked, the version does not publish, the tools it made are removed, and the teacher sees which item failed, for example "Notes (page 1)" or "Flashcards".
- **The same check for class shares** (`classes/sharing:share`, now an action). Sharing a notebook with a class checks the sources and read only tools the class would see; if one fails nothing is shared and the teacher is told which. The class sees only sources and tools that passed. A tool or source added after sharing waits, the share picker says how many, and "Check and share them" checks them. Set work (quizzes and papers set for a class) is unchanged in this commit.
- **Mock fixtures.** The output check's mock key names where the material is going: `{ pack: <pack name>, tool }` or `{ shared: <notebook name>, tool }`. In tests a pack named "Flagged Biology" has its flashcards blocked, and a notebook named "Flagged notes" its summary.
- **Schema.** `notebooks.profileId`, `sources.profileId`, `outputs.profileId`, `cards.profileId`, `coursePacks.createdBy` and `coursePacks.masterNotebookId` become optional; new optional `notebooks.organisationId` (index `by_organisation`), `coursePacks.notebookId` (index `by_notebook`), `sources.copiedFrom`, `sources.minorCheck` and `outputs.minorCheck`. Nothing existing is rewritten. A pack published before this commit has no copy, so it reads as unpublished until it is published again; TEACHER_MODE has been off in production, so this only touches local data.

With nothing typed: For your exam (matched to "What are you studying for", by words such as O Level, A Level, ACCA), Popular (by sales) and New.
- **The reader** is view only: selecting, copying, cutting, dragging, the context menu and printing are off, and the buyer's display name and email run faintly across every page and every bought tool. The copies keep no files, so there is nothing to download. A bought source now has a Read button that opens its pages with previous and next. This deters casual copying; it cannot stop a photo of the screen.
- **Offline.** The app keeps no notebook pages offline yet (vite.config.ts caches only the app shell). When offline notebooks arrive, a bought or rented one must store its access end and drop its pages then.
- **After buying** the buyer gets their own notebook linked to the listing (`notebooks.listingId`), read like a course pack: Taku answers from it, it shows in Ask Taku's notebook picker marked Bought, its tools and review cards work as a pack's, and the notebook and its list entry say "Bought" or "Rented until <date>". A rental reads until `endsAt` exactly: every read compares the time, and a step scheduled for that moment marks it ended so open pages change at once. An ended rental shows "Your rental has ended." with Renew, which rents again into the same notebook. A deleted bought notebook can be opened again from the listing while access lasts.
- **Payments.** A payment provider interface (`convex/market/payments.ts`) with one provider, `demo`. Checkout shows "Demo payment, no real money", offers EcoCash, OneMoney, InnBucks and card, and the buyer chooses whether the payment succeeds or fails; a failed one takes nothing and the checkout closes. The first real provider will be **Paynow** (EcoCash, OneMoney, InnBucks, Omari, ZimSwitch, Visa and Mastercard; official Node SDK `paynow` on npm): it starts a payment, then confirms it through Paynow's result callback, which will take the place of the demo's `settle`. **Stripe** may come later for buyers outside Zimbabwe. No real provider code or keys are in the repository.
- **Money.** Takudzidza keeps 20% and the seller gets 80% (`split`, rounded to the cent, the seller's share being the remainder). Every sale or rental writes a ledger row: buyer, listing, seller, amount, commission, seller share, provider, status. A refund writes a reversing row with negative amounts. A seller's share is pending for 7 days (a refund counts from its sale's time, so a refunded sale never shows as owed), then owed. The owner records payouts by hand (amount, date, reference) and downloads the full ledger as CSV; nothing is paid out automatically. The owner can refund within 7 days of a sale; access ends and the seller's pending share drops.
- **Settings > Money.** Everyone: purchases and rentals with their state, end dates and receipts (reference, method, date). Sellers: owed, pending and paid out, every sale and rental, and payouts. Adults who do not sell yet: "Become a seller".
- **Deletion.** A buyer's deletion removes their notebooks, checkouts and reviews (and takes their stars off the listing); their purchases and ledger rows stay, shown as "deleted account". A seller's deletion stops their listings selling (`sellerGoneAt`) and drops any waiting version; people who bought keep their copy and rentals run to their end, because the listing's notebook belongs to no account. The seller's row keeps their name and payout number while money is owed, so the owner page still shows the balance; once the owner marks the last of it paid, both are cleared. A seller with no sales has them cleared at once.
- **Tests only.** Two internal functions refuse outside mock mode, like the mock outbox: `market/purchases:endRentalSoon` and `market/money:ageSalesForTest`, so journeys can watch a rental end and pending money become owed.
- **Mock fixtures.** The output check's key at approval is `{ listing: <title>, tool }`. In tests a listing named "Biology for grown ups" has its summary blocked.
- **Schema.** New tables `sellers`, `listings`, `checkouts`, `purchases`, `ledger`, `payouts`, `reviews` and `reports`; optional `notebooks.listingId` with an index `by_listing`. Nothing existing is rewritten.
