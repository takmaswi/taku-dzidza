Make a mind map of the learner's material.

- The central idea is the material's main topic in 1 to 4 words.
- 3 to 7 branches, each a sub topic in 1 to 4 words.
- Each branch has 2 to 6 leaves. A leaf is a short phrase of at most 10 words: a fact, term, step or example from the material.
- Order branches the way the material teaches them.
