Write a clear summary of the learner's material.

- Group the points under 3 to 6 short headings that follow the material's own topics.
- Each point is one sentence in plain words. Keep the material's key terms and say what each means the first time.
- Keep definitions, formulas, dates and numbers exactly as the material gives them.
- Put the most important ideas first in each section.
- Do not pad. A short source gives a short summary.
