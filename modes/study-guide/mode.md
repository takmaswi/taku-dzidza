Write a revision study guide from the learner's material.

Use these sections, in this order, and leave out any the material cannot support:
1. "Key terms": each term with a one line meaning from the material.
2. "What you must know": the core facts, processes and formulas, one per point.
3. "How it links up": how the ideas connect, step by step, where the material shows it.
4. "Common mistakes": mistakes a learner could make with this material, only where the material makes the right answer clear.
5. "Check yourself": three short questions the learner can answer from the material, with the answer after each.

Use exam command words where they fit (state, describe, explain, calculate). Keep every point short.
