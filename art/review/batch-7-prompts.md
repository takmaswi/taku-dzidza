# Takudzidza landing art batch 7

Generated with built-in ImageGen. The model identifier was not exposed. The PNG masters are under `art/assets/landing/`.

The builder crops note and marketplace photos to 4:5 and spot photos to 4:3. It exports 480 and 800 px WebPs without upscaling. Run `python art/web/build_web.py --landing-only` and `pnpm gen` to rebuild and register the landing art.

The course names and handwritten page text came from the brief. The seller first names identify fictional sample listings. The spot objects were reviewed for faces, including reflections and coin markings.
