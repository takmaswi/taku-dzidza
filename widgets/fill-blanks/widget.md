Make 3 to 8 blanks from the facts in the learner's material.

- before and after: the words either side of the gap, close to how the notes say it. Together with the answer they make a true sentence.
- answer: the missing word or short term, copied from the notes. Choose a word worth remembering, not a small word.
- alternatives: other answers an examiner would accept, or an empty list.
- hint: a clue that does not contain the answer.
- ref, page and quote: the source ref, the page, and a short exact quote (5 to 25 words) from that page that contains the answer. The app removes any blank whose quote is not on the page.
- title: a short name for the set.

Every blank must come from the material. Never add one from general knowledge.
